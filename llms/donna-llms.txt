# Donna

> Donna is the only AI assistant built for field sales teams. She prepares reps for meetings, takes notes online or in person, auto-updates the CRM, drafts follow-ups, and gives managers full visibility into the field. Built by Dealside BV. Trusted by 100+ organizations and rated 4.9/5 on G2.

Donna works conversationally (voice, text, or call), is hands-free for reps on the go, and integrates with Salesforce, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics 365, HubSpot, Outlook, and Google Calendar, including custom CRM objects and fields. Customers typically report about 75% less admin time, up to 10x higher CRM adoption, and roughly 20% higher sales conversion. Donna is ISO 27001 certified and fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and SOC 2. Data is encrypted in transit and never used to train AI models. Most teams are fully onboarded in under two weeks.
